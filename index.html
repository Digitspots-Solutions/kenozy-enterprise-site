About Us

KENOZY ENTERPRISE is a leading manufacturing company based in Obio Akpor, Port Harcourt. We pride ourselves on delivering high-quality manufactured products that meet international standards while serving the Nigerian market. Our facility at Rumukrushi is equipped with modern equipment and operated by skilled professionals committed to excellence.

We understand the unique needs of Nigerian businesses and provide manufacturing solutions that combine quality, affordability, and reliability. Our commitment to customer satisfaction and continuous improvement has made us a trusted partner for businesses across various sectors.
